Saved Dashboards are the central hub for product analytics in Klaritics. A dashboard is a collection of widgets you pin from various analyses, such as charts, funnels, retention curves, and metric cards. Once built, a dashboard gives your team a single screen to monitor product health, track experiments, and share insights.

## What you can do with dashboards

- **Pin widgets** from any analysis and arrange them visually
- **Resize and reorder** widgets to highlight the most important metrics
- **Share dashboards** with teammates based on their roles
- **Export results** for presentations or offline review
- **Configure alerts** on individual widgets so you are notified when metrics change

## Dashboard building blocks

Dashboards are made of widgets. Widgets are visual representations of analyses you create elsewhere in Klaritics. You can learn more about widget types and how to configure them in the Widgets section.
