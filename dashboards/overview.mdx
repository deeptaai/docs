Saved Dashboards are the central hub for product analytics in Klaritics. A dashboard is a collection of charts you pin from various analyses, such as charts, funnels, retention curves, and metric cards. Once built, a dashboard gives your team a single screen to monitor product health, track experiments, and share insights.

## What you can do with dashboards

- **Pin charts** from any analysis and arrange them visually
- **Resize and reorder** charts to highlight the most important metrics
- **Share dashboards** with teammates based on their roles
- **Export results** for presentations or offline review
- **Configure alerts** on individual charts so you are notified when metrics change

## Dashboard building blocks

Dashboards are made of charts. Charts are visual representations of analyses you create elsewhere in Klaritics. You can learn more about chart types and how to configure them in the Charts section.
