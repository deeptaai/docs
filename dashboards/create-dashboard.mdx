Creating a dashboard in Klaritics lets you assemble the most important analytical widgets into a single view. Start with an empty board, pull in visualizations from your saved analyses, arrange them to tell a clear story, and save the layout for your team.

## Create a new dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new dashboard">
    Go to **Dashboards \> Saved Dashboards** and click **New Dashboard**. Give it a descriptive name, such as "Q3 Activation Metrics" or "Weekly Product Health".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin widgets from analyses">
    Open any analysis you have already built, such as a chart, funnel, or retention report. Click **Pin to Dashboard** and select the dashboard you just created. Repeat for each widget you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange the layout">
    Drag widgets to reposition them. Resize each widget by dragging its corners so the most important metrics get the most space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the dashboard">
    Click **Save** to store the layout. Your dashboard is now available to anyone you share it with.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can pin the same analysis to multiple dashboards. This is useful when a single metric matters across different team views.
</Tip>
