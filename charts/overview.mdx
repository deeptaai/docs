A **chart** is a saved analysis. Every analysis type in Klaritics produces one — a funnel, a retention curve, a stickiness distribution, a pivot table, an engagement matrix.

**Saved Charts** is the listing of every chart you are allowed to access in a project.

---

## Charts are independent of dashboards

This is the rule that explains most of the behavior in this section.

Charts can be pinned to dashboards, but they do not belong to them. The two are separate resources with separate permissions, in both directions.

<Note>
  Deleting a dashboard does **not** delete the charts it contained. They remain in Saved Charts until you delete them explicitly.
</Note>

Being able to edit a dashboard does not let you edit the charts on it, and chart-level permissions are always checked before chart data is exposed.

---

## What the listing shows

The listing displays only the charts you are allowed to access, based on each chart's visibility.

| Visibility | Who sees the chart |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Private** | Owner only |
| **Restricted** | Owner and explicitly shared users |
| **Project-wide** | Project users, subject to their chart project-level permission |
