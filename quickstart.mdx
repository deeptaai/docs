This walks through going from nothing to a working dashboard: deploy Klaritics, instrument an app, send an event, and build something from it.

<Note>
  You need two values throughout: a **`project_id`** identifying your project, and a **`server_host`** pointing at your deployment. Both come from your Klaritics instance once it is running.
</Note>

---

## 1. Deploy Klaritics

Klaritics runs inside your own infrastructure. Follow the [self-hosted deployment guide](/deployment/self-hosted) to stand up your environment.

You finish this step with a `project_id` and a `server_host` URL.

## 3. Initialize and send an event

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web">
    ```js
    import Klaritics from "@deeptaai/klaritics-web-sdk";

    Klaritics.init("YOUR_PROJECT_ID", {
      server_host: "https://YOUR_SERVER_HOST"
    });

    Klaritics.logEvent("ADD_TO_CART", {
      userId: "user@example.com",
      value: 1299,
      item: "Sony Head Phone 1201"
    });
    ```
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Other platforms">
    The initialization shape is the same everywhere — a project identifier plus a server host — with platform-specific setup covered in each guide:

    [Android](/sdk/android) · [iOS](/sdk/ios) · [React Native](/sdk/react-native) · [Flutter](/sdk/flutter) · [Kotlin Multiplatform](/sdk/kmp)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Name events for what happened in your product — `ADD_TO_CART`, `ProductClicked` — and keep the naming consistent from the start. See [Event naming](/sdk/event-naming); the `$` prefix is reserved.
</Tip>

---

## 4. Confirm the event arrived

Open [Real-Time Events](/data/real-time-events) and look for your event. It shows the incoming event stream along with the properties attached to each one, so you can verify the payload is what you expected before building anything on top of it.

If nothing appears, check that `server_host` is reachable from the client and that `project_id` matches your project.

---

## 5. Turn it into something useful

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build an analysis">
    Create a [Chart Analysis](/analysis/chart-analysis) from your event, or jump straight to a [Funnel](/analysis/funnels) if you have two or more steps to measure. Saving the analysis produces a **chart**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it">
    [Pin the chart](/charts/pin-to-dashboard) to the project Overview, an existing dashboard, or a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Set the dashboard to Restricted or Project-wide so your team can see it. See [Sharing](/dashboards/sharing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an alert">
    Configure [alerts](/settings/alerts) on the chart so changes reach you without checking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  In a hurry? Skip the manual build and apply a [template](/templates/overview). Pick Product KPIs, supply one active event, and you have a populated dashboard immediately.
</Tip>
