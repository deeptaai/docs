The Feature Adoption template is designed for product teams shipping new capabilities. It gives you a focused dashboard to measure how quickly users discover, try, and continue using a new feature after launch.

## What is included

This template creates a dashboard with widgets tailored to adoption tracking:

- **First-time usage** showing how many users tried the feature for the first time each day.
- **Adoption rate** as a percentage of your active user base.
- **Repeat usage** to see if users come back after their first try.
- **Trend comparison** against previous feature launches or baseline behavior.

## Customizing the template

After applying the template, map the widgets to the specific events that represent your new feature. You can also add [Cohorts](/analysis/cohorts) to compare adoption across user segments, or set up [Alerts](/alerts/overview) to notify you when adoption crosses a threshold.
