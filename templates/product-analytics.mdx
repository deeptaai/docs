The Product Analytics template is the broadest starting point in Klaritics. It gives you a dashboard of core product metrics so you can understand overall usage, growth trends, and engagement patterns without building charts from scratch.

## What is included

This template creates a dashboard with widgets covering the most common product questions:

- **Active users** over time, including daily, weekly, and monthly views.
- **Event volume trends** to see which actions users take most.
- **Top events** ranked by frequency.
- **User growth** to track how your user base is expanding.

## Customizing the template

After applying the template, you can edit any widget, change date ranges, add filters, and rearrange the layout. You can also add [Alerts](/alerts/overview) to key widgets so you are notified when metrics shift.
