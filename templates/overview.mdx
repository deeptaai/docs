Templates in Klaritics give you a pre-built starting point for common analytics needs. Instead of creating charts and dashboards from scratch, you can apply a template and start analyzing your data immediately. Each template includes a curated set of widgets and layouts designed for a specific use case.

## How templates work

When you apply a template, Klaritics creates a new dashboard populated with the template's default widgets. You can customize every widget, add new ones, remove the ones you do not need, and adjust the layout. Templates are a starting point, not a locked configuration.

<Tip>
  Apply a template even if you already have dashboards. It is often faster to adapt a pre-built layout than to build a new one from scratch.
</Tip>
