The Product KPIs template distills your product's performance into a single, scannable dashboard. It is designed for teams who need a quick daily or weekly read on the metrics that matter most, without digging through multiple reports.

## What is included

This template creates a compact dashboard of headline metrics:

- **Active user counts** including daily, weekly, and monthly active users.
- **Key event totals** for your most important product actions.
- **Growth indicators** such as new user signups and return user rates.
- **Engagement signals** to show whether users are staying active over time.

## Customizing the template

The template gives you a solid default layout. You can swap in your own key events, adjust date ranges, resize widgets for emphasis, and add [Alerts](/alerts/overview) so you are notified when a headline metric moves outside your expected range.
