Klaritics is a self-hosted product analytics platform that provides end-to-end analytics across user management, data, analysis, dashboards, templates, integrations, and alerts. Deploy the full platform inside your own infrastructure and analyze product usage without sending data to a third party.

## How Klaritics works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Instrument your product">
    Add the Klaritics SDK for [Web](/sdk/web), [Android](/sdk/android), [iOS](/sdk/ios), [React Native](/sdk/react-native), [Kotlin Multiplatform](/sdk/kmp), or [Flutter](/sdk/flutter) and start sending events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your event taxonomy">
    Organize incoming events and attributes in the [Event Taxonomy](/data/event-taxonomy) module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build analyses and charts">
    Create chart analyses, funnels, retention, cohorts, and other analyses, then pin them to dashboards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share dashboards and set alerts">
    Save dashboards for your team and configure alerts on any chart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Klaritics v1.0.0 ships all modules listed above. Compass, Session Engagement, User Flows, and Session Replays are on the roadmap and are not part of this release. See the [release notes](/release-notes) for details.
</Note>
