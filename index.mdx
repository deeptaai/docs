Klaritics is a product analytics platform that runs **entirely inside your own infrastructure**. Your event data never leaves your environment, and there is no third-party vendor between you and your users' behavior.

## How Klaritics works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Instrument your product" icon="code">
    Add a Klaritics SDK and start sending events. Every SDK uses the same pattern: supply your `project_id` and `server_host`, then log events.

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Web" href="/sdk/web" />
      <Card title="Android" href="/sdk/android" />
      <Card title="iOS" href="/sdk/ios" />
      <Card title="React Native" href="/sdk/react-native" />
      <Card title="Flutter" href="/sdk/flutter" />
      <Card title="Kotlin MP" href="/sdk/kmp" />
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Organize what you collect" icon="database">
    Events arrive and appear in [Real-Time Events](/data/real-time-events) within moments. Use [Event Taxonomy](/data/event-taxonomy) to name, categorize, and control the visibility of every event you track.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask questions of the data" icon="chart-line">
    Build a funnel, measure retention, check how many days a week users come back, or compare feature breadth against depth. Each saved analysis becomes a **chart**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assemble and share" icon="users">
    Pin charts to dashboards or the project Overview, then share them as Private, Restricted, or Project-wide. Set alerts so you hear about changes without looking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can build

Klaritics groups everything you can create into three categories, reached from **Create Chart or Dashboard**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Behavior Analytics">
    Analyses built around how users actually behave over time.

    | Analysis | Answers |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | [Funnels](/analysis/funnels) | Where do users drop out of a sequence? |
    | [Retention](/analysis/retention) | Do users come back after a first action? |
    | [Stickiness](/analysis/stickiness) | How many days per week or month do users return? |
    | [Engagement Matrix](/analysis/engagement-matrix) | Which features are broad, deep, both, or neither? |
    | [Impact Analysis](/analysis/impact-analysis) | What changes after a user first does something? |
    | [Pivot Tables](/analysis/pivot-tables) | How does a metric slice across several dimensions? |
    | [User Sessions](/analysis/user-sessions) | How long and how often do users engage per session? |

    <Note>
      **User Paths** and **Compass** appear in this category but are not available in the current release.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Charts">
    [Chart Analysis](/analysis/chart-analysis) builds a flexible metric from your events — choose a measurement, filter it, break it down, and compare periods.

    Eight visualizations are available:

    ```text
    Line     Stacked Line     Bar     Stacked Bar
    Column   Stacked Column   Pie     Metric
    ```
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Default Templates">
    Pre-built dashboards that need only an event or two to become useful.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Flow Analysis" href="/templates/flow-analysis">
        Multi-step journeys and drop-off.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Feature Adoption" href="/templates/feature-adoption">
        Discovery, engagement, retention for one feature.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Product KPIs" href="/templates/product-kpis">
        Headline growth and engagement metrics.
      </Card>
      <Card title="User Activity" href="/templates/user-activity">
        Active users, first-time usage, distribution.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Governing access

Klaritics separates two layers of access control.

| Layer | Controls | Where |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Roles** | What a person can do across the platform | [Settings → Users & Permissions](/settings/roles) |
| **Resource permissions** | Who can open, edit, or share a specific dashboard or chart | [Permissions](/dashboards/permissions) |

<Warning>
  Dashboards and charts carry **independent** permissions. Being able to edit a dashboard does not grant access to the charts pinned to it.
</Warning>
